How this works: this file lives outside the shipped app (Productivity/, not part of the build). When you ask for a new feature, Claude adds a row here as Not Started. The moment Claude actually starts working on something, it flips to In Progress and shows up in the "Working on now" banner at the top of the page — so you always know what's active without asking. When something ships, its row moves to Done in Recently Shipped / the Timeline view, and any verification screenshot Claude used to confirm it lands in Productivity/screenshots/ rather than a temp folder that gets wiped.
