# CFB Dynasty Hub — 1.0 🎉

The spreadsheet is officially retired. **1.0 is here** — the app is stable, the rough edges are sanded down, and syncing your dynasty is now something you genuinely can't get wrong.

---

## ⭐ The headline: sync whenever you want

You used to have to sync at *just* the right moment — and tiptoe around the coaching carousel so you didn't scramble your data. **Not anymore.**

- The moment a season ends, the app **locks it**. Sync again later — deep in the offseason, mid-carousel, on Signing Day — and your finished season stays exactly as you left it.
- **Changing schools is automatic.** Your finished season stays with your old school; your new school picks up at Week 0. No special order, no danger window.
- You just get the normal "Synced" confirmation. No way to overwrite your good data.

**The two syncs that matter each year:** at the **End of Season Recap** (locks your record, bowl result, and awards) and one step later at **"Players Leaving"** (see below).

---

## 🆕 New in 1.0

- **Transfers & Departures** — a new **"Left the program"** list shows who declared for the **NFL** (with their projected draft round), who **graduated**, and who **transferred out — and why**. Sync at the offseason's *"Players Leaving"* step to capture it.
- **Edit Skill Points & XP** — the player editor can now set a player's **Skill Points** and **XP Points** directly, right alongside their ratings.
- **Check for updates on startup** — turn it on in Preferences and the app tells you when a new version is out. (It's how you got this one!)
- **Quick Help, rebuilt** — the in-app help (now "Quick Help") opens with a clear, plain-English **"When to sync"** guide so you never miss a moment that matters.
- **Updated User Manual** — rewritten around the new sync behavior, with the sync cheat sheet right up front.

## ✨ If you're coming from an older version

- **Clickable team names everywhere** — click any team, anywhere, for an instant card with its record, ranking, stats, and top players.
- **NCAA Hub Statistics** — national team and player leaderboards across all of FBS.
- Roster **NIL** column + team spend, conference-champion trophies, and more.

## 🐛 Fixed

- **Coach alma maters are correct now.** They were showing the wrong school — that's fixed across the board.
- Players/coaches that showed initials instead of their real portrait now render properly.
- High-school recruits no longer show up in the transfer list with FCS logos.
- Fixed a rare stuck/unscrollable screen when opening cards on top of each other, and centered the team pop-up cards.

---

## ⚠️ Important — do this once after updating

A couple of 1.0's improvements need to read your save fresh to take effect:

**➡️ After you update, open your dynasty and hit Sync once.**

That's all — it refreshes your current season so the coach alma-mater fix and the new Transfers & Departures data show up. Your older seasons stay exactly as they are.

---

## 📥 How to update

Just run **CFB Dynasty Hub Setup 1.0.0.exe** — it updates the app in place. **You do *not* need to reinstall the image data**; your existing graphics pack still works. (If Windows shows a blue SmartScreen notice, click *More info → Run anyway* — normal for a small indie app.)
