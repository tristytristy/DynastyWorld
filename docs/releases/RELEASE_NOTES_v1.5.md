# CFB Dynasty Hub — 1.5 🏈

A big feature update to 1.0. **1.5** brings player **trading cards**, a grown-up **Media Hub**, a cleaner Team Hub, and an all-black look — and it's a normal in-place update, so your graphics come with it (no separate image-data download).

---

## ⭐ Player Trading Cards

Open any player and hit the new **Card** tab for a clean, shareable trading card — themed to that player's real team colors, with their portrait, overall, position, and jersey number.

- **Roll over the card for Edit / Export.** *Export* saves it as a PNG. *Edit* opens the tools.
- **Drop in your own photo** — pick a file, or reuse a screenshot you've already tagged to that player in the Media Hub — then **zoom and drag to frame it**.
- **Pick the stats** that headline the bottom (up to four), with smart defaults per position.
- **Hover preview** — just rest your cursor on a player's name anywhere and their card pops up. Turn it on/off and set the delay under **Preferences → Player card preview**.

## 🖼️ Media Hub — now its own section

The media gallery is out of Team Hub and up top as its own **Media Hub**, and it grew up:

- **Drag & drop** photos straight from your computer onto the grid to add them, with a **progress bar** for big batches.
- **Batch edits** — click **Select**, tick a bunch of shots, and **set their game all at once** (no more one-by-one for 20 screenshots from the same game), or **delete them together**.
- **Drag to reorder** — uploaded out of order? Rearrange them and it sticks.
- Photos always open **centered**, and deleting media (or a whole dynasty) now removes the actual files from your computer — nothing left lying around.

## 🧭 A tidier Team Hub

The Team Hub tab row went from **eleven tabs to six** — related pages now share a tab with a small toggle:

- **Roster** ⇄ Transfers
- **Schedule** ⇄ Rivalries
- **Statistics** ⇄ Trends
- **Honors** = Season Awards ⇄ Weekly Honors
- Plus **Overview** and **History**.

## 🖤 All-black theme

Dark mode is now a true black base. Your team color shows up only where it should — buttons, tabs, accents, and your cards — never as a blue wash across the background.

## ✨ Also new since 1.0

- **Coaching Tree** on Coach Hub — where your former assistants landed, growing each season.
- **Rivalries** — all-time head-to-head series records vs every opponent.
- **Season-in-Review Yearbook** — a shareable per-season recap you can export.
- **Player Development Tracker** — a player's overall arc across every synced season.
- **Coach Résumé** and **Global Search** (Ctrl/Cmd + K to jump to any player, coach, or team).
- Preferences now open **collapsed** for a tidier panel.

---

## ⚠️ Important — sync once after updating

Some of this reads your save fresh to take effect.

**➡️ After you update, open your dynasty and hit Sync once.** Your older seasons stay exactly as they are.

## 📥 How to update

Just run **CFB Dynasty Hub Setup 1.5.0.exe** — it updates the app in place, graphics and all. You do **not** need to reinstall the image data. (If Windows shows a blue SmartScreen notice, click *More info → Run anyway* — normal for a small indie app.)
